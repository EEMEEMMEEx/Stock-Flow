## Design Context

### Users
Business owners and staff managing inventory, processing sales (POS), and tracking assets. They require a reliable, efficient, and visually clear interface to handle day-to-day operations.

### Brand Personality
Phantom Magnetar: Modern, High-performance, Reliable.
The name suggests something powerful, fast, and sophisticated.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Current**: High-end glassmorphism with neon accents and 3D effects. Uses a dark/light theme with a Thai-friendly font (Prompt).
**Target**: Maintain the "premium" feel while ensuring it doesn't look like generic "AI slop". Prioritize clarity and efficiency for a business-critical application.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over Decoration**: While aesthetics are important, the information (stock levels, prices, transaction status) must be the hero.
2. **Speed & Efficiency**: Interactions should be fast, especially in the POS system.
3. **Consistency**: Use the established design tokens and CSS variables to ensure a cohesive experience across all 15+ pages.
4. **Resilience**: Handle edge cases (missing data, offline states) gracefully.
